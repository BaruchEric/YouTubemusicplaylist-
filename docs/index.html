CrateSync

Playlists of the music you own, sent to YouTube Music.

CrateSync reads a folder of music files in your browser, lets you build playlists from it, and matches each song to its video on YouTube Music. One click then creates the playlist on your YouTube account, private, with the songs in your order. When the playlist changes, the next push adds, moves and removes only what changed.

  • Your library stays in your browser. CrateSync has no database of its own.
  • A push never touches a song you added on YouTube yourself.
  • Google Drive can hold a copy of the library, so a second device can pull it.

The running copy at music.beric.ca is private. The source is on GitHub.
