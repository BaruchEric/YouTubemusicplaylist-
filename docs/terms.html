What CrateSync is

CrateSync is a web app that builds playlists from the music files you own and sends them to YouTube Music. It is a personal project, offered free of charge. Its developer runs one copy at music.beric.ca for private use and may give or take away access to that copy at any time.

YouTube

CrateSync uses YouTube API Services to find the videos for your songs and to create and change playlists on your YouTube account. By using CrateSync you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

A push changes a playlist on your YouTube account. It adds videos, moves them, and removes videos that an earlier push added. CrateSync shows the counts before it starts. What you push is your decision.

YouTube limits how many searches and changes CrateSync can make in a day, and every user of this copy shares that limit. Do not try to get around it.

Your Google account

You choose what CrateSync may reach: YouTube, Google Drive, both or neither. You can take that access back at any time on the Google security settings page. The privacy policy says what CrateSync reads and keeps.
